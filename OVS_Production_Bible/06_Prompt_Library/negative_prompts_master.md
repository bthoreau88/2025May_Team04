# OVS Standard Negative Prompts — Master Block

Copy the full block into every prompt, then remove categories that don't apply to the specific shot.

---

## Full Master Block

```
no visible AI artifacts, no morphing geometry, no extra fingers, no limb doubling, 
no face flicker, no texture swimming, no background warping, no HDR look, 
no oversaturated color, no color grading presets, no stock footage lighting, 
no ring light, no beauty dish fill, no lifestyle photography look, no modern clothing, 
no digital devices, no LED lights, no contemporary vehicles, no brand logos, 
no plastic surfaces unless period-appropriate, no horror jump-scare framing, 
no comedy pratfall staging, no music video aesthetics, no fashion editorial posing, 
no social media vertical framing, no shaky handheld unless specified, 
no drone shot unless specified, no slow-motion unless specified, 
no text overlays, no subtitles in frame, no lens flare, no anamorphic streak unless specified
```
