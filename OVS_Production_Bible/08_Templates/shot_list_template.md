## Frame Shorthand
- `ECU` — Extreme close-up
- `CU` — Close-up
- `MCU` — Medium close-up
- `MS` — Medium shot
- `MLS` — Medium long shot
- `LS` — Long shot / wide
- `EWS` — Extreme wide / establishing
- `OTS` — Over the shoulder
- `POV` — Point of view
- `INS` — Insert (object detail)

## Movement Shorthand
- `LOCK` — Locked camera, no move
- `PUSH` — Slow push in
- `PULL` — Pull back
- `PAN L/R` — Pan left or right
- `TILT U/D` — Tilt up or down
- `RACK` — Rack focus (specify: rack from [A] to [B])
- `HANDHELD` — Handheld (must be justified)
